Physical explanation:
The band-edge and Fermi-referenced models use the same linear density of states and finite-temperature Fermi function, but different lower integration limits. The band-edge model integrates from the Dirac point E = 0, while the Fermi-referenced model starts from the current Fermi level EF. As EF increases with positive doping, the band-edge model includes an increasing number of occupied states between 0 and EF, so the correction factor C = n_band / n_Fermi increases.

Operating-point result:
At EF = 0.09875 eV, n_band = 8.35e11 cm^-2 and n_Fermi = 3.25e11 cm^-2, giving C = 2.570. Thus, the band-edge model overestimates the carrier density by a factor of 2.57. Since mu = 1/(e n rho) at fixed sheet resistivity, the mobility is underestimated by the same factor: 2.44e4 cm^2 V^-1 s^-1 becomes 6.27e4 cm^2 V^-1 s^-1 after correction.

Quantified advice:
For G07 sensor benchmarking at the operating point, use the Fermi-referenced carrier count; the band-edge model overestimates carrier density by 2.57x and underestimates the extracted mobility by 2.57x.
